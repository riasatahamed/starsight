StarSight v31.8.8

Artwork stability fix:
- Constellation artwork anchors now reuse the exact projected x/y positions of
  their matched HIP/star objects whenever available.
- This prevents a second independent RA/Dec -> Alt/Az calculation from drifting
  relative to the WebGL star renderer.
- The fallback astronomical projection remains only for anchors that cannot be
  matched to a currently projected HIP/star object.
- AR constellation rendering remains disabled for performance.
