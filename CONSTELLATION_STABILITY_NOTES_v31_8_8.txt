StarSight v31.8.8

Stability rollback:
- Removed the experimental celestial mesh artwork warp.
- Restored the stable three-anchor screen-space affine registration.
- Two-anchor artwork uses similarity registration.
- Artwork mode no longer draws the constellation line network underneath it.
- Constellation line vertices are quarter-pixel snapped to reduce Canvas antialiasing shimmer.
- Synthetic Stellarium helper stars are excluded from the visible star LOD lists.
- AR remains constellation-free for performance.
- Normal sky map constellation default remains OFF.
